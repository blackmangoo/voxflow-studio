## 2. Structural & Motion Principles
- Full-viewport subtle vertical architectural column hairlines (`6-column architectural grid`).
- Dual-tone rim lighting in Three.js WebGL (Cerulean `#007BA7` key light + Neon Rose `#FF2D95` rim light + Platinum liquid chrome torus knot / gimbal).
- GSAP ScrollTrigger scrub animations: parallax 3D camera, horizontal kinetic text scrub, clip-path card reveals, and interactive circular arrow `( -> )` CTAs.
